## Brand & Style
The design system positions the digital portfolio not as a static archive of artifacts, but as an interactive spatial exhibition and continuous cinematic set. The brand persona is avant-garde, authoritative, unapologetically structured, and culturally acute. It targets executive design leadership, high-fashion creative directors, cutting-edge tech founders, and contemporary digital art institutions.

The visual methodology fuses **High-Contrast Brutalism** with **Cinematic Editorial Luxury**. It rejects generic dashboard conventions, template carousels, and passive scrolling in favor of tactile gravity, tectonic scale shifts, and camera-like viewport choreography. The environment feels physically present: vast atmospheric planes of muted violet collide with sharp typographic structures and neon energy vectors, balancing deliberate quietude with explosive graphic velocity.

## Colors
The palette operates like stage lighting in a physical theater space:

- **Atmospheric Medium (Lavender `#BFA8F7`, Lavender Dark `#8B6DE2`):** Defines the environmental container, ambient scrims, and soft focal fields. It provides a tactile, non-white substrate that relieves eye fatigue while maintaining high-art sensitivity.
- **Architectural Mass (Tomato Red `#F4513A`, Tomato Dark `#D83822`):** Used for load-bearing anchors, monolithic project cards, aggressive hover states, and critical actions. It holds decisive gravitational pull across the canvas.
- **Kinetic Energy (Chartreuse `#D8FF38`, Chartreuse Light `#E8FF70`):** Applied strictly to dynamic vectors, focus rings, timeline cursors, active mode indicators, and micro-trajectories. Never used for large passive backgrounds.
- **Structural Spine (Black `#0B0B0B`):** Establishes structural grid lines, stark boundary borders, baseline marks, and razor-sharp typographic metadata.
- **Breathing Space (Warm White `#F7F3EC`):** Inserted through high-fashion editorial editorial inserts, paper-like project dossier backdrops, and luminous reversed typography.

Surfaces default to ambient lavender layers bathed over dark foundations. Color is not decorative; it establishes physical hierarchy, focal friction, and spatial momentum.

## Typography
Typographic orchestration is strictly tri-cameral:

1. **Architectural Mass (Syne):** Sculptural, wide, and aggressive. Headings operate as spatial geometries rather than plain text. Rendered in all-caps or tightly tracked lowercase for project titles, scene identifiers, and numeric indices.
2. **Editorial High-Fashion Resonance (Newsreader italic substitution for Instrument Serif):** Provides literary nuance, quiet subversion, and human vulnerability. Used for pull quotes, project philosophies, personal reflections, and artistic statements.
3. **Information Density & Precision (Inter):** Clinical, utilitarian, and neutral. Used for project specifications, execution dates, scope breakdowns, body narratives, and interactive UI telemetry.

All text aligns to an inviolable vertical rhythm. Display headings must clip and interact with adjacent modules rather than float on detached cards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a continuous 12-column architectural matrix with zero dead scroll. Every scroll tick or swipe shifts viewport perspectives or advances content along explicit coordinate rails.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 56px (`3.5rem`) canvas margins, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Content elements snap to visible 1px hairline guide rules (`#0B0B0B` with 15% opacity or pure solid on active zones).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Secondary telemetry folds into sliding bottom sheets or drawer panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters. Spatial panning collapses to stacked horizontal bands with dynamic bottom transport bars.

Negative space is intentional and pressurised: large vacant lavender fields are abruptly interrupted by full-bleed tomato red case studies or razor-thin diagnostic data rows.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons conventional blurred drop shadows entirely in favor of **Camera Depth Planes** and **Solid Geometric Offset Layers**:

1. **Surface 0 (Atmospheric Floor):** Lavender `#BFA8F7` environmental substrate with subtle grain and ambient vignetting.
2. **Surface 1 (Floating Planes):** Warm White `#F7F3EC` or Lavender Dark `#8B6DE2` panels framed by crisp 1.5px solid `#0B0B0B` borders.
3. **Surface 2 (Tectonic Portals):** Tomato Red `#F4513A` sculptural masses projecting hard, unblurred drop-casts: `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #0B0B0B`.
4. **Surface 3 (HUD & Telemetry):** Fixed camera frame elements, timecode coordinates, and cursor crosshairs hovering over the canvas in Chartreuse `#D8FF38` and Black `#0B0B0B`.
5. **Backdrop Blurs:** Reserved exclusively for navigation modals and scene-shift transitions (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over 80% opacity lavender scrims).

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly **Sharp (Level 0)**. Every card, button, container, tag, and modal exists with 0px border radii to echo cinematic film frames, architectural blueprints, and gallery gallery plinths. 

Corners meet at razor-sharp 90-degree junctions. Contrast and delineation are achieved strictly through line weight (1px to 2px solid strokes), high-contrast field fills, and deliberate negative space.

## Components

### Action Triggers & Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button:** Solid Tomato Red `#F4513A` box with 1.5px `#0B0B0B` border, sharp corners, and high-contrast Black `#0B0B0B` typography in `label-caps`. Hover activates an instantaneous translation (-3px, -3px) casting a solid Chartreuse `#D8FF38` under-block (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0B0B0B`).
- **Ghost Dossier Trigger:** Transparent background, 1px `#0B0B0B` outline, Inter uppercase metadata. On hover, fills solid Black `#0B0B0B` with Warm White `#F7F3EC` text.

### Gallery Index Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Rectangular tags with 1px `#0B0B0B` outline. Background in Chartreuse `#D8FF38` for live artifacts, Warm White `#F7F3EC` for archival works. Typography uses `meta-technical`.
- **Category Filter Pills:** Sharp block pills with left-aligned coordinate markers (e.g., `[01/DEV]`, `[02/SPATIAL]`). Active states toggle to Tomato Red `#F4513A` with Warm White text.

### Case Study Showcase Cards
- Asymmetrical architectural blocks. Media frames maintain a strict 16:9 or 4:5 cinematic aspect ratio bounded by 1.5px `#0B0B0B` borders.
- Metadata strip sits directly beneath the artwork: `meta-technical` label on the left, high-contrast title in `headline-md` (Syne) on the right.
- On card hover, a Chartreuse `#D8FF38` lens frame overlays the imagery with camera corner crop marks.

### Diagnostic Lists & Ledger Rows
- Interactive index list dividing entries with continuous 1px `#0B0B0B` rule-lines.
- Columns conform to the 12-column grid: `Col 1-2: Year`, `Col 3-7: Project Title & Client`, `Col 8-10: Disciplines`, `Col 11-12: Arrow Action`.
- Row hover triggers full-bleed background inversion from Lavender `#BFA8F7` to Warm White `#F7F3EC` or Tomato Red `#F4513A`.

### Selection Controls & Inputs
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Raw, square, 16x16px boxes framed with 1.5px `#0B0B0B` stroke. Checked state fills solid with Chartreuse `#D8FF38` containing a solid black center dot or square.
- **Input Fields:** Flat Warm White `#F7F3EC` or transparent lavender input strips bounded by a bottom-only 2px `#0B0B0B` rule. Floating labels in `meta-technical`. Focus state turns the baseline rule into Tomato Red `#F4513A` accompanied by an inline Chartreuse cursor pip.

### Filmic Viewport HUD (Bespoke Product Component)
- Fixed corner anchors display current spatial coordinates, active film roll timecodes, and soundscape toggles.
- Cursors mutate contextually into custom crosshairs, drag lenses, and projectile viewing arrows.

Raymond — Contemporary Visual Research & Monumental Color Fields PARIS 1991 — ACTIVE ATELIER NEW YORK & PROVENCE
MATERIAL INTEGRITY / 01

Painting is neither illustration nor illusion. It is mineral matter deposited with violent precision upon coarse woven linen, demanding that the viewer confront mass, gravity, and duration.

In an era dominated by dematerialized surfaces and fleeting retinal stimuli, Raymond constructs towering physical monuments. Each canvas functions like a tectonic fault line: thick layers of burnt sienna, cold carbon impasto, and raw ochre pigments are carved into with heavy steel trowels, creating reliefs that physically cast shadows as gallery light shifts throughout the diurnal cycle.

The works refuse narrative decoration. Instead, they operate as raw environmental presences that command silent contemplation and recalibrate human physical scale.

SECTION CRITIQUE: L'ESPACE BRUT PARIS, 2026

T he artisan status claimed by Raymond is not an ironical nod to historical guild traditions; it is a literal ethical commitment to manual labor in an age of frictionless optical simulations.

Every canvas begins with the stretching of unprimed raw flax, pulled taut over heavy kiln-dried ash stretchers built in the atelier. The ground is treated not with industrial gesso, but with custom bone-glue size and precipitated chalk, allowing the microscopic teeth of the fibers to grip the first tectonic pass of paint.

When carbon black touches the ground, there is no preliminary sketch, no pentimento masked under subsequent layers. The action is absolute.

What distinguishes Raymond from the historic abstract expressionists of the mid-20th century is his refusal of theatrical emotionalism. There is no bravura performance here; the gesture is slow, architectural, almost geological in its patient deposition.

The resulting planes feel like fragments torn from a collapsed temple or sea walls encrusted with mineral deposits. To stand before them is to experience an ontological reset: human scale is gently restored, the noise of external communication evaporates, and matter asserts its indisputable presence.

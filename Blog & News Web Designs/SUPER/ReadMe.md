# SUPER — Editorial News & Magazine

## 1. Project Brief
**SUPER** is an avant-garde digital editorial platform and cultural publication. It rejects conventional SaaS patterns, e-commerce templates, and generic blog layouts in favor of an art-directed, cinematic luxury reading experience. Combining the tactility of independent print magazines with the dynamic precision of digital media, SUPER delivers critical intelligence across architecture, culture, industrial design, emerging technology, and contemporary philosophy.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Art Direction
* **Aesthetic Direction:** Cinematic Editorial Noir / Dark Luxury Publication / Experimental Brutalism.
* **Mood & Tone:** Confident, restrained, intellectual, provocative, and physical.
* **Surface Finish:** Tactile 3% procedural fractal noise overlay simulating uncoated heavy card stock and print grain. Flat architectural planes with strict 0–4px corner radii—no glowing drop-shadows, gradients, or glassmorphic blurs.
* **Layout Structure:** Strict 12-column editorial grid with asymmetric multi-column spans (e.g. 5-column feature stories, 3-column dispatches, 4-column cultural analyses) framed by crisp 1px structural hairpins.

## 4. Typography System

### Font Pairings
* **Display / Headlines:** `Clash Grotesk` (or geometric editorial grotesk, such as `Syne` / `Cabinet Grotesk`).
  * *Scale:* Fluid editorial sizing scaling up to `11.5vw` on desktop (`16vw` on mobile).
  * *Tracking:* Ultra-tight tracking (`tracking-tighter` / `-0.04em`).
  * *Line Height:* Razor-sharp `0.85` leading for massive impact.
* **Body & Longform:** `General Sans` (or refined neo-grotesk / `Inter`).
  * *Weights:* 300 Light to 400 Regular for effortless long-session reading.
  * *Line Height:* Balanced `1.6–1.7` for breathability.
* **Technical Labels & Metadata:** Monospaced / Micro Neo-Grotesk.
  * *Styling:* All caps, `0.7rem–0.75rem`, wide letter spacing (`tracking-widest` / `+0.12em` to `+0.2em`).
  * *Function:* Visual punctuation, telemetry stamps, dispatch coordinates, and curation dates.

### Signature Layered Typographic Treatment
Major editorial statements and hero headlines utilize dual-layer pixel-aligned typographic composition:
1. **Back Layer:** `color: transparent`, with `1px solid #66473B` stroke offset by `4px 4px`.
2. **Front Layer:** Solid `#EBDCC4` primary tone, locked in identical alignment, tracking, and leading.

## 5. Component Patterns & Visual Cadence
1. **Minimal Editorial Masthead:** High-restraint header with bold brand mark, clean section ledger, minimal search trigger, and hairline edition acquisition CTA.
2. **Asymmetric Hero Spread:** Oversized title intersecting with an edge-anchored brutalist desert architectural monument, flanked by micro-metadata and curatorial notes.
3. **Partitioned Category Bar:** Flat 1px border dividers with active terracotta underlines—zero pill buttons or rounded badges.
4. **Dispatches & Dossiers Grid:** Asymmetric cards pairing high-contrast chiaroscuro photography with structured specs (material tests, telemetry logs, acoustic studies).
5. **Typographic Manifesto Spread:** High-contrast full-bleed negative space celebrating editorial conviction (*"WE ARE LIVING THROUGH THE NEXT VERSION"*).
6. **Continuous Rotating Colophon Badge:** Subtle circular SVG stamp running a continuous 12s rotation near the dispatch input.

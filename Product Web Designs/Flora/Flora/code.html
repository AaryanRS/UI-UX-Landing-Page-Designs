STAGE 05 · ASTRONOMICAL RHYTHMS

THE THREE CYCLES OF THE SEED YEAR

Soil temperature dictates germination velocity, not calendar dates. Synchronise your indoor propagation and direct drills with regional frost transitions.

CYCLE 01 FEB 15 — APR 01

COLD PROPAGATION

Sow indoors under bottom heat: nightshades, alliums, and long-season brassicas. Water with ambient rainwater to maintain root mantle temperatures above 18°C.

CYCLE 02 APR 02 — MAY 20

DIRECT DRILL & HARDEN

Direct seed root vegetables, early peas, and cool salads as the loam warms to 10°C. Harden seedlings through graduated north-breeze exposure over ten days.

CYCLE 03 MAY 21 — JUN 30

SETTLED BED TRANSPLANTS

Set out vigorous transplants into deeply tilled raised beds after final frost date. Top-dress with aged leaf mould and interplant companion tagetes.

VERIFIED HEIRLOOMS
WHAT QUALIFIES AN ACCESSION FOR THE FLORA ARCHIVE? +
Every variety in our custody has maintained genetic stability through a minimum of fifty years of open-pollinated field cultivation without hybrid cross-contamination. We grow isolations with certified physical barriers and hand-pollinate select umbels to ensure trueness to type.
HOW ARE GERMINATION PERCENTAGES VALIDATED? +
We perform four-replicate 100-seed rolled rag assays in temperature-controlled chambers according to ISTA (International Seed Testing Association) protocols. Results are recorded per lot number and stamped onto each individual packet prior to archival sealing.
ARE ALL SEEDS UNTREATED AND NON-GMO? +
Strictly yes. None of our inventory receives synthetic fungicide dips, neonicotinoid seed dressings, or pelletised plastic treatments. We believe the seed coat must respire naturally with the living microbiome of your native garden soil.
WHAT IS THE VIABLE STORAGE LIFESPAN OF A SEALED PACKET? +
When maintained in our unbleached envelopes in a cool, dark, desiccant-stabilised environment (under 12°C and 30% relative humidity), nightshades and brassicas remain viable for 4 to 6 years, alliums for 1 to 2 seasons, and cucurbits up to 8 seasons.

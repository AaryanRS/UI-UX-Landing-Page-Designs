SECTION 04 · MATERIAL MATRIX

Stoneware earth pigments & geological stone.

Materials are classified strictly by origin, compressive yield and kiln behaviour. We accept no synthetic resins, cementitious binders or superficial sealants.

MAT-01 · TERRACOTTA ARGILLA
High-iron alluvial clay gathered from riverbed floodplains in Impruneta. Coarse grog added at 28% by dry weight to prevent shrinkage distortion across high-fire reduction cycles.
FE₂O₃ > 8.4% FIRE CONE 9 / 1,260°C
MAT-02 · TRAVERTINO ROMANO
Sedimentary calcium carbonate extracted from quarry beds in Tivoli. Visible geothermal vacuole channels are left un-grouted, establishing friction interlock between dry-stacked layers.
CACO₃ 98.2% DENSITY 2.45 G/CM³

Extrusive volcanic stone selected for anchoring plinths where maximum shear resistance is necessary. Honed with water-fed diamond pads to a 200-grit matte tooth.

STUDIO PHILOSOPHY

Form exists as the equilibrium between compressive dead weight and planetary pull.

Every sculpture generated in our workshop operates as an independent dry-stack puzzle. We reject bolts, concealed rebar, epoxies and modern elastomer adhesives. If a work cannot stand purely through its mass distribution and the friction of its milled stone beds, the geometry is discarded and returned to clay slip.

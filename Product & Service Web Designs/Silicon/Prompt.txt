# SILION — HUMAN-GRADE AI LANDING PAGE PROMPT SPECIFICATION

## System & Creative Intent
Create a premium, cinematic landing page for an enterprise AI infrastructure company called **Silion**.
The objective is to establish an atmospheric, high-end, and mathematically rigorous visual presence that communicates human-grade causal reasoning, deterministic verification, and intelligence grounded in real-world telemetry — steering completely clear of generic SaaS gradients, playful illustrations, or standard chatbot tropes.

---

## 1. BRAND & IDENTITY SPECIFICATION
- **Product Name:** Silion
- **Positioning:** Human-Grade Reasoning / Sovereign Cognitive Infrastructure
- **Wordmark Treatment:** Minimal, restrained geometric typography in Geist / Inter (`SILION // SYS.AI // V4.2`), accompanied by an ultra-thin technical badge or monospaced version string.
- **Tone:** Scientific, expensive, mysterious, minimal, and computationally authoritative.

---

- **Background Tones:**

- **Atmospheric Layering:** Hairline coordinate grid overlays, subtle radial dark falloff vignetting, and zero garish gradient blobs.

---

## 3. HERO COMPOSITION & INTERACTIVE VISUALIZATION
- **Volumetric Particle Human Mind (Three.js / WebGL):**
  - Synthesize ~14,000 volumetric luminous points forming an anatomical human cranium, temple, cheekbone, eye socket, jaw, and neck profile that dissolves into atmospheric neural dust.
  - **Dynamic Traveling Energy Pulse:** A localized luminescence wave that sweeps periodically down facial coordinates (temple → cheek → jaw), exciting particles from deep blue into electric violet and cyan-white.
  - **Mouse Parallax & Inertia:** Subtle responsive tilt (3–8px) toward the user's cursor with micro-shimmer and organic drift.
- **Hero Headline & Layout:**
  - Left-aligned headline: `"Human-Grade Reasoning."` with negative letter tracking and tight line height.
  - Sub-copy: *"Engineered for multi-step causal reasoning directed directly in enterprise telemetry, unstructured operational records, and sovereign real-time context."*
  - Dual technical CTAs: Primary pill button `"Deploy Silion Engine ⚡"` and ghost border button `"Explore Architecture Audit →"`.
- **Cyclic Message Transition (Lower Right):**
  - Smooth cycling text transition (blur + fade ease) between core brand axioms:
    1. *"Built Into Every Decision."*
    2. *"Grounded In Your Own Data."*
    3. *"Verifiable Multi-Step Chains."*
    4. *"Zero Speculative Hallucination."*
- **Technical HUD Telemetry:**
  - Minimal monospace coordinate markers: `VECTOR_LAT: 47.6062° N`, `VECTOR_LNG: -122.3321° W`, `ENTROPY: 0.0028 ± 0.0001`, `CYCLE_TIME: 14.2ms`.

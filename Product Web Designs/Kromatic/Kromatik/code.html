MANUFACTURE DISCIPLINE

SEVEN STAGES OF MECHANICAL REFINEMENT

Every component is shaped from solid billet stock, hand-chamfered with gentian wood paste, and validated across 360 continuous hours in 5 thermal conditions.

STAGE 01 / BILLET CUTTING
Low-speed electrical discharge machining of titanium bridges and high-lead brass wheel blanks
42.0 HOURS
STAGE 02 / ANGLAGE & POLISHING
Manual filing of interior angles to 45° chamfers followed by diamond micro-mesh and boxwood lapping
28.5 HOURS
STAGE 03 / TOOTH PROFILE TRUING
Cycloidal tooth curve correction with optical projector comparison against master template plates
14.0 HOURS
STAGE 04 / HAIRSPRING PINNING
Laser attachment of terminal stud followed by microscopic hand-bending of the Breguet overcoil curve
18.0 HOURS
STAGE 05 / 5-POSITION POISING
Static and dynamic balancing on ruby knife edges; mass adjustment via gold micro-screws
22.0 HOURS
STAGE 06 / OBSERVATORY TESTING
Continuous recording in thermal chambers cycling between 8°C, 23°C, and 38°C under barometric variations
360 HOURS
STAGE 07 / CASING & FINAL LEDGER
Inking of the individual certificate, pressure testing to 5 ATM, and wax sealing in chronometer case
08.0 HOURS

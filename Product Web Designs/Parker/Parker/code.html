01 / The Mechanics of Capillary Balance

Every pen is an engine that meters fluid against air.

Mass-manufactured pens rely on plastic feeds with wide injection molding tolerances, requiring thick chemical solvents to regulate surface tension. Parker tools its internal breather channel from natural hard ebonite with sixty-four sub-millimeter fins, ensuring ink moves solely through physical atmospheric exchange without skips, pooling, or railroading.
